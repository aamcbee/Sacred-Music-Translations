## Pronunciation Respelling Presentations

We include here PowerPoint presentations that have the lyrics to children's songs in other languages, with pronunciation respelling for English-speaking children above and below the lyrics. 
These are designed to help teach songs to bilingual congregations, and can be used as a slide-presentation on a computer/TV or printed and put in a binder. 
While younger children will not be able to read these lyrics, older children will and can help younger children to learn the songs in another language.

One recommendation that works well in our primary--where junior and senior are combined--is asking a member of the senior class to hold and flip through the songs (or advance the slides), while you as the singing time leader still do hand motions to help the younger children engage.
